Mobile Tools

Two free sound instruments made to be played on a phone. They run entirely in your browser: no accounts, no installs, nothing uploaded.

Matrices

Pattern launcher + visualiser

Eight voices with five patterns each. Tap a block to loop it. Patterns run at independent lengths and never re-align, so you get polyrhythm and drift rather than a grid. A generative visualiser is driven by whatever is playing, and the canvas doubles as a touch surface for effects, patterns and geometry.

Four packs change what it sounds and looks like: Lattice (clicks, sines and sub), Sferic (shortwave static and carriers), Cantus (long sustained tones in a key) and Strata (granulated physical models).

Getting started: open the page and tap once. It starts playing straight away and moves through scenes on its own. Roll the dice to reroll patterns and sounds, long-press a block to pin it, and save scenes to chain them into a performance. On iPhone, turn the silent switch off.

FieldLooper

Field recording into drones, through effects you build

Record the world with your phone's microphone and stretch it into drones with Tape, Spectral or Granular stretch. Build your own FX chain from 31 effects: add, remove and drag them into order, each with its own wet/dry. Record your moves on any effect and they loop too. A generative sequencer walks a resonator through a scale.

Getting started: open it in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen so it runs full-screen and offline. Allow the microphone, record a take, drag the stretch up, then open the FX tab and add an effect or apply a preset. Your work autosaves, and you can export a .fieldlooper project or use Performance Record to capture everything you play as a 24-bit WAV.

Good to know

  • Both are built for phones and work in any current browser with Web Audio. Headphones or a decent speaker help: the low end sits below what a phone speaker can reproduce.
  • Settings, scenes and recordings are stored in your browser on that device only. Clearing site data clears them.
